FOLLOW GALTSYSTEMS

How can I follow new posts?

Use a feed reader to see new GaltSystems essays alongside the other sites you follow. No email address or account on this site is needed.

How does RSS work?

A feed reader checks this site's feed for new posts and collects them in one place. The feed uses Atom, a format supported by RSS readers.

  1. Open your preferred feed reader.
  2. Choose its option to add or follow a website.
  3. Paste the feed address below and confirm in your reader.

Open the raw feed (XML) ↗

The raw feed may look like code in your browser. That is normal: it is designed for your feed reader.
